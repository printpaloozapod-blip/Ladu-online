Asad's rules

  • Two dice. Split them between pieces however you like, but each die is spent whole.
  • Getting out: a 6 on either die brings a piece in — double 6s bring two in. Any double can bring one in too, but then the whole roll is spent.
  • Extra rolls: roll again when you roll a 6 and after every kill. But a 6 rolled right after a kill earns nothing. Doubles alone earn nothing.
  • Forced kill: if a kill is sitting there with ONE die, you must take it — even your own partner. A kill that needs two moves is your choice, never forced. Multiple kills available? You pick which.
  • Backward kill: roll a 1 and an enemy sits exactly one square behind you — you kill backwards. Not from an entry square.
  • Safe squares: only the four entry squares (★). Safe for everyone standing on them.
  • Stacks are walls: 2+ of your pieces on a square = locked. Nobody can kill it, land on it, or pass it — not even you. Land exactly on it or unlock it.
  • Door lock: your stack alone on someone's entry square means they can't bring pieces in.
  • Home column: the 12 square on top is shared and exposed; the 5 squares under it are private. From the 12, any dice combo totaling 6 takes the piece home. Overshoot can't be used on a piece.
  • Second hand: get all 4 home and you sit one round out, then your turns move your partner's pieces.
  • First team with all 8 pieces home wins.
